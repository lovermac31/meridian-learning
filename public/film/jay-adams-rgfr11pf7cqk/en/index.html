What is this film about?

In this 60-second film, Jay Adams, an American educator and innovator, explains why real education should build thinkers, not just test-takers. He introduces Jurassic English, which builds communication, critical thinking and English proficiency through engaging lessons, and WorldWise Learning, which brings education and intelligent technology together for institutions, educators and professionals.
