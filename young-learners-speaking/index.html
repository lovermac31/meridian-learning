One path from 9 to 18

Three age stages, one 60-minute correction loop

Ages 9–11 · CEFR A2–B1

Sentence & Story Confidence

Enjoy speaking instead of fearing it: clear pronunciation habits, everyday vocabulary, and describing and explaining in connected sentences.

Ages 11–14 · CEFR B1–B2

Structured Explanation & Early Band Habits

From short, safe answers to real ideas: opinions with reasons, precise vocabulary, and the first exam-style tasks, tracked on the four public criteria every lesson.

Ages 15–18 · Band 6.5–8.5 targets

Full IELTS Speaking Parts 1–3 Readiness

Part 3 argument, advanced grammar under pressure and full timed mocks, so your teen walks into the test, and university interviews, calm and ready.

Every 60-minute lesson

  1. Warm-up: settle in and set today’s one target.
  2. Guided speaking: the real practice, with the teacher noting key sentences live.
  3. Feedback & re-try: hear what to fix, fix it, say it again, better.
  4. Take-home habit: one small thing to practise before next time.

Every lesson tracks the four public IELTS Speaking criteria.
